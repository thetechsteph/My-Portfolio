About Me

I'm Stephanie Dominic, a frontend developer, STEM educator, and technology enthusiast interested in the intersection of technology, creativity, and problem-solving. My journey into tech started at a young age, and what began as curiosity has grown into a continuous pursuit of learning, building, and exploring what's possible with technology.
